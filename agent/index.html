Agent

A personal calendar assistant. It is used by its owner only and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

Agent looks for things to do — concerts, films, exhibitions, markets — and when one seems a good fit, it adds it to its owner's Google Calendar as an invitation. The owner accepts it, declines it, or ignores it, and Agent learns from the answer which suggestions are worth making.

How it uses Google Calendar

With the permission of the Google account that signs in, Agent:

  • creates, updates and deletes the suggestion events on the calendars that account owns;
  • reads the events of the calendars that account can see, to avoid suggesting something at a busy time and to read the answers to its invitations.

It asks for nothing else: no email, no contacts, no files.
